# Quality Model

Noema separates **conformance** from **quality**.

- Conformance asks whether a project is structured, governed, and auditable according to the protocol.
- Quality is demonstrated through declared quality claims and proportional evidence.

Evidence may be deterministic, runtime, human, model-based, external-source, or observational. A claim may remain `UNASSESSED`; Noema must not convert missing evidence into a pass.

No universal aggregate score is defined in RC0.
